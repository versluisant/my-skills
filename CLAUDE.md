This repo holds my personal skills and agents, loaded as the `vas` plugin.

- Skills live in skills/<name>/SKILL.md, agents in agents/<name>.md.
- The `name:` field must match the folder or file name.
- Keep skills short. Do not add steps I did not ask for.
- After changing a skill or agent: show me the diff, then commit with a
  message that says what changed and why, then push.
- Do not commit anything outside skills/, agents/, the manifests,
  README.md and CLAUDE.md without asking.
